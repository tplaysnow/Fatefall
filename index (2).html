Welcome to Fatefall.

A little magic for your next session. Every roll happens right here in your browser, even offline.

Make your roll

  • Quick roll: choose a quantity and modifier, then tap a die. The large Roll button repeats your current setup.
  • Build a pool: tap dice to add them. Use the small minus buttons to remove dice, then roll the whole pool. The modifier applies once to the total.
  • Advantage / disadvantage: available for a single d20. Roll two and keep the higher or lower result. Ties keep one die.

Speak the language of dice

Try 2d6 + 1d8 + 3, d20 - 2, 2d20kh1 + 5 (keep highest), or 4d6kh3 (ability score). Use kl to keep the lowest. Up to 30 dice per roll; modifiers from −999 to +999.

The fine print of fate

A d100 is rolled as two ten-sided dice: tens and units. 00 + 0 means 100. Other pairs read normally; 00 + 7 means 7. Natural 1 and 20 badges use the kept die of a single-d20 check, before modifiers. Your table decides what they mean.

The presets are ready-made dice formulas. Apply any character bonuses or other rules that your table uses.

Stay in the moment

Space / R roll the current setup. 1–7 quick-roll d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, and d100. Sound is optional. Reduced motion skips the animation.

Dice use your browser’s secure random generator with equal chances for each face. The animation reveals the result; it doesn’t decide it. History and preferences stay in this browser when storage is available.
